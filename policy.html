Terms & Privacy

Version 1.0 · applies to the app and the admin panel

What BomberMan does

BomberMan fires repeated HTTP requests to third-party endpoints so an SMS, call, email or WhatsApp ping lands on the subject you enter. We do not own the endpoints; delivery depends on the upstream provider.

Account

One account per person, max two devices. Coin balance, license and history live on our servers. Sharing an account may get it limited.

Coins

Coins are consumed per request and are non-transferable. Failed requests are refunded automatically. Trial coins are granted once per account.

Licenses

Monthly, yearly and lifetime codes bind to the first account that activates them. Revoked or expired codes stop working immediately.

Coupons & giveaways

Per-user limits apply. Duplicate or automated redemption can be reversed.

Fair use

Per-tool cooldowns, daily caps and a per-tool kill switch exist to keep upstream providers from blocking us.

Privacy

We store name, phone, email, age, device fingerprint and job history. Endpoint URLs stay server-side. No data is sold.

Refunds

Coins only. There is no cash refund once coins are credited.
